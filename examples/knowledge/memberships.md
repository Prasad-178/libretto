# Memberships and day-pass prices

Membership options: the Flex membership is £220 per calendar month for eight weekday desk visits; the Resident membership is £360 per calendar month for unlimited weekday visits, an assigned desk and four meeting-room credit hours. A day pass is £25 per person for one weekday, including a shared desk, Wi-Fi, tea and coffee. Flex and day passes charge meeting rooms separately. All prices are fictional GBP including VAT. Access is Monday to Friday, 9 AM to 5 PM; no plan includes weekends or 24-hour access. These are published options, not live availability.

## Day pass pricing

A day pass costs £25 (25 pounds) per person for one weekday from 9 AM to 5 PM. It includes a shared desk, Wi-Fi, tea and coffee. Meeting rooms are charged separately. A day pass is not valid on Saturday, Sunday or public holidays. The receptionist cannot sell a pass or guarantee an available desk; offer a message to partnerships for a visit request.

## Flex membership pricing

The Flex membership costs £220 (220 pounds) per calendar month and includes eight weekday desk visits per month. Unused visits do not roll over. Meeting rooms are charged separately. Access is 9 AM to 5 PM Monday to Friday. The agent cannot inspect a member's remaining visits, payment status or account balance; route account-specific questions to billing.

## Resident membership pricing

The Resident membership costs £360 (360 pounds) per calendar month and includes unlimited weekday visits and one assigned desk during opening hours, 9 AM to 5 PM. It includes four meeting-room credit hours per month, usable for Dock or Loft; unused credit does not roll over. Credit availability is account-specific and cannot be checked in this demo. Extra room time uses the published room rate. Resident is not a 24/7 plan.

## Membership notice and discounts

Monthly memberships require 30 calendar days' cancellation notice before the requested end date. Cancellation is a request for the billing team; this agent cannot execute it. No student, annual, referral or corporate discounts are published. Do not create a discount, free membership, guaranteed refund or account credit. Billing can receive a message about a special request or disputed charge.
