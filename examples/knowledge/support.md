# Support

## Support hours

Support is available Monday to Friday, 9 AM to 5 PM London time. There is no live support on weekends: Saturday and Sunday are closed. This fictional company does not offer 24-hour emergency support.

## Supported environments

Northline's developer tools support current stable releases of Linux, macOS, and Windows. Browser tools support current Chrome, Firefox, Safari, and Edge. Support can help with installation, API connection errors, and product documentation. It cannot access a visitor's real accounts in this demo.

## Billing and refunds

Billing handles invoices, subscription questions, and requests to discuss a refund. The knowledge base does not specify refund eligibility, prices, discounts, account balances, or a guaranteed callback time. A receptionist must not invent these details; offer a message to billing.

## Partnerships and workshops

Partnerships handles integration proposals, company collaborations, and workshop enquiries. Workshops cover API design, testing, and developer tooling. Schedules and prices are not published in this demonstration knowledge base.
