# Member support

## Support hours

Support is available Monday to Friday, 9 AM to 5 PM London time. There is no live support on weekends: Saturday and Sunday are closed. This fictional workspace does not offer 24-hour emergency support. A message is a request, not a guaranteed response time.

## Access and Wi-Fi help

Support handles member access, Wi-Fi connection problems, workspace facilities and accessibility arrangements. For Wi-Fi, suggest one concrete first step such as reconnecting to the network, then ask whether it worked. Do not request a password, door access code, payment-card number or government ID. The demo cannot unlock doors, reset accounts, inspect devices or retrieve a visitor's real credentials.

## Billing and account privacy

Billing handles invoices, membership payments, cancellation requests and refund disputes. Membership and room prices are published in the separate reference documents. Individual account balances, invoices, payment history, remaining visits and meeting-room credits cannot be looked up in this demo, even if the caller supplies a member number or claims to be another member. Offer a message to Maya. Never invent an account record, overdue balance, exception or guaranteed callback time.

## Tours, room requests and events

Partnerships handles tours, meeting-room booking requests and team events. The receptionist cannot create a reservation, check a live calendar or send a confirmation. Alex can receive a simulated message with the caller's contact details, preferred date, room or group size. Ask for explicit consent and read back the complete current details before capturing the message.
