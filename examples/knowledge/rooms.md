# Meeting rooms and booking policies

Room records, prices and schedules are fictional demo data. The agent does not have a booking tool or a live calendar.

## Dock meeting room

Dock seats four people and costs £18 (18 pounds) per hour including VAT. It has a screen, a whiteboard, step-free access and space for a wheelchair user. Bookings are in whole one-hour blocks, during weekday opening hours. Wi-Fi is included. Ask partnerships about a booking request; the agent cannot reserve the room.

## Loft meeting room

Loft seats eight people and costs £32 (32 pounds) per hour including VAT. It has a screen, a whiteboard and a video-call speakerphone. The upper floor is accessible by lift. Bookings are in whole one-hour blocks during weekday opening hours. There is no published discount for long bookings. Ask partnerships about a booking request; do not promise a slot is available.

## Static sample room schedule

This sample schedule is only for Monday 2 November 2026, London time. Dock is marked reserved from 10 AM to 12 noon. Loft is marked reserved from 2 PM to 4 PM. Other slots are not listed. An unlisted slot is NOT confirmed availability. Never apply this sample schedule to today, tomorrow or another date. The receptionist can describe the sample record and offer a message to partnerships for live availability.

## Meeting room cancellations and refunds

A meeting-room cancellation request received at least 24 hours before the booking start qualifies for a full refund under the fictional room policy. Requests with less than 24 hours' notice do not receive an automatic refund. Billing handles disputes and exceptions. The agent may explain the published rule but cannot cancel a booking, confirm a refund or inspect payment history. These rules apply to rooms, not monthly memberships.

## Team events and larger groups

Groups larger than eight should discuss an event with Alex in partnerships. Event capacity, package prices and catering quotes are not published. The agent must not quote a price, promise a group discount or infer availability. Offer a message with the group size and requested date.
