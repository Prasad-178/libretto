# Northline Workspace

Northline Workspace is a fictional coworking space used only in the Libretto demo. All staff, addresses, prices, policies and caller profiles are invented test data. Jamie is the front-desk receptionist. The workspace offers day passes, monthly desk memberships, meeting rooms and small team events. This is not a real venue or business service.

## Reception hours and location

Reception and workspace access are Monday to Friday, 9 AM to 5 PM London time. Saturday, Sunday and public holidays are closed for all demo plans. There is no 24-hour access. The fictional address is 42 Example Lane, London. The holiday closures in the sample calendar are 25 December 2026 and 1 January 2027. A request for a tour can be left with partnerships; the agent cannot book or guarantee a tour slot.

## Accessibility and facilities

The fictional building has step-free entry, a lift, an accessible toilet and an accessible meeting room called Dock. Wi-Fi, drinking water, tea and coffee are included in desk plans. There is a quiet work area, but no guarantee it will be empty. Visitors may request a written follow-up instead of a phone conversation. Leave a message with support for specific access arrangements. Parking spaces, pet policies and overnight storage are not specified; do not invent them.

## Team directory and routing

Sam Rivera in support handles member access, Wi-Fi, facilities and accessibility questions. Maya Chen in billing handles invoices, membership payments and refund disputes. Alex Morgan in partnerships handles workspace tours, team events and meeting-room booking requests. Ask a narrow clarification when the requested team is ambiguous. Confirm the specific destination before attempting a transfer.

## What this demo can do

The agent can answer from these public reference documents, simulate a transfer, or capture a confirmed message in the current session. It cannot look up a member account, read invoices, access a live calendar, reserve a room, charge a card, process a refund or send an email. Nothing is delivered to a real person. All captured messages disappear when the connection ends unless the visitor exports their session. Do not claim an appointment, booking or payment exists.
