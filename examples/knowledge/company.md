# Northline Studio

Northline Studio is a fictional software company used in the Libretto demo. It builds developer tools and helps small teams design internal software. It offers product engineering, API integrations, and technical workshops. This demonstration is not a real company service.

## Office hours and location

The office and reception are open Monday to Friday, 9 AM to 5 PM London time. The office is closed on weekends, including Saturday and Sunday, and on public holidays. The fictional office is at 42 Example Lane, London. Remote meetings are available by arrangement.

## Accessibility

The fictional office has step-free access, a lift, and an accessible meeting room. Visitors can request written follow-up instead of a phone conversation. For specific accessibility arrangements, leave a message for support.

## Contact policy

The receptionist can simulate a transfer to support, billing, or partnerships. If someone is unavailable, it can capture a name, callback number, recipient and message for the current demo session. Nothing is sent to a real person. Demo messages disappear when the conversation ends, unless the visitor downloads their session.
